# WATTWISE – User Quickstart Guide

## 1. Introduction

Welcome to WATTWISE!

WATTWISE is a household electricity monitoring
and budget management platform that helps users
understand their electricity consumption,
estimate electricity bills and explore
energy-saving opportunities.

This guide explains how to get started.

## 2. Getting Started

### Step 1 – Open WATTWISE

1. Open the WATTWISE application in your browser.
2. Wait for the Overview Dashboard to load.
3. Explore the electricity consumption summary.

You can view the available consumption and
estimated billing information.

### Step 2 – Manage Your Appliances

1. Navigate to Appliance Management.
2. Explore the available household appliances.
3. Check their wattage and operating hours.
4. Change appliance ON/OFF states where available.

The application uses appliance information
to calculate estimated electricity consumption.

### Step 3 – Monitor Your Electricity Budget

1. Open the Budget Planner.
2. View your estimated electricity bill.
3. Adjust the available usage or budget inputs.
4. Observe the updated estimates.

The estimated bill depends on appliance usage,
billing duration and the configured electricity tariff.

### Step 4 – Explore Additional Features

WATTWISE provides additional modules:

- Energy Analytics:
  Explore electricity consumption charts.

- Smart Recommendations:
  View suggestions for reducing energy usage.

- Emergency Mode:
  Explore simulated electricity management scenarios.

- Settings:
  Access available application settings.

## 3. Understanding Electricity Calculations

WATTWISE estimates energy consumption using:

Energy (kWh) =
(Wattage × Daily Operating Hours × Number of Days)
÷ 1000

Estimated Electricity Bill =
Total Energy Consumption × Electricity Tariff

These values are estimates and may differ from
actual electricity bills.

## 4. Saving and Restoring Information

Version A uses browser LocalStorage for supported
application data persistence.

Information may remain available after refreshing
the browser on the same device and browser.

Clearing browser storage may remove saved
application information.

## 5. Recovering from Mistakes

If you accidentally change an appliance status
or an editable input:

1. Return to the relevant module.
2. Correct the input or appliance status.
3. Review the updated calculation.

If the application behaves unexpectedly,
refresh the browser and check the saved state.

## 6. Important Information

- WATTWISE is a prototype.
- Electricity readings are calculated estimates.
- No physical electrical appliances are controlled.
- Emergency Mode is simulated.
- Historical charts may contain demonstration data.

Thank you for using WATTWISE!
