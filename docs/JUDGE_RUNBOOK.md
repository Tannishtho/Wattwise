# WATTWISE – Judge Runbook

## 1. Project Overview

WATTWISE is a smart household electricity monitoring and
budget management platform designed to help users understand,
monitor and optimise their electricity consumption.

The application uses configurable appliance data, electricity
usage calculations and simulated scenarios to demonstrate
how households can manage their energy consumption.

## 2. 60-Second Demonstration Script

### Step 1 – Open the Application (0–5 seconds)
- Launch the WATTWISE application.
- Navigate to the Overview Dashboard.
- Show the main electricity consumption indicators.

Expected Result:
The dashboard displays electricity usage and estimated
billing information.

### Step 2 – Appliance Management (5–15 seconds)
- Open Appliance Management.
- Display the available household appliances.
- Switch an appliance ON or OFF.

Expected Result:
The appliance status changes and its contribution to
calculated electricity consumption is updated.

### Step 3 – Budget Planner (15–25 seconds)
- Navigate to the Budget Planner.
- View the estimated electricity bill.
- Modify the available usage or budget inputs.

Expected Result:
The estimated bill reflects the updated calculation.

### Step 4 – Energy Analytics (25–35 seconds)
- Open Energy Analytics.
- Display consumption charts and usage breakdowns.

Expected Result:
The charts visualise the available electricity
consumption data.

### Step 5 – Smart Recommendations (35–45 seconds)
- Open Smart Recommendations.
- Review the energy-saving suggestions.

Expected Result:
The application displays recommendations intended
to encourage efficient electricity usage.

### Step 6 – Emergency Mode (45–55 seconds)
- Open Emergency Mode.
- Demonstrate the simulated Power Cut feature.
- Select the required appliances.

Expected Result:
The application demonstrates a simulated power
management scenario.

### Step 7 – Final Overview (55–60 seconds)
- Return to the Overview Dashboard.
- Summarise how WATTWISE helps users understand and
  manage household electricity consumption.

## 3. Important Technical Notes

- Electricity calculations are performed locally.
- Appliance consumption is calculated using wattage
  and daily operating hours.
- The Power Cut feature is simulated and does not
  control physical electrical appliances.
- Historical information is demonstration data.
- No physical IoT integration is implemented in Version A.

## 4. Demonstration Limitations

WATTWISE is a prototype intended to demonstrate
household energy monitoring and management concepts.

The displayed electricity consumption and bills
are estimates based on configured inputs, not
live readings from an electricity meter.
